# Monty Hall Problem Simulation

This project programmatically simulates the famous Monty Hall probability puzzle to visually and mathematically prove that switching doors yields a higher win rate than staying with the initial choice. 

Through rapid iterations and statistical tracking, the simulation acts as a practical demonstration of conditional probability in action.
